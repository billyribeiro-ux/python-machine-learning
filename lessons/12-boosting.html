Module 12 · Machine Learning

XGBoost & LightGBM Alpha

Gradient-boosted trees are the workhorse of tabular alpha. We train, validate, and interpret them — and feed their predictions back into the backtest engine.

🚧 Full lesson in progress

The course is being written module-by-module for maximum depth. This lesson's detailed outline is below so you can see exactly what it will cover — and the underlying quantlab package code it builds on already exists and is tested. Modules 0–4 are complete and runnable; work through those first.

What this lesson will cover

Why boosted trees for tabular features

Nonlinear interactions, robustness to feature scale, and strong baselines vs deep nets on tabular market data.

XGBoost vs LightGBM

API differences, speed, categorical handling, and when to pick which.

Validation done right

Early stopping on a time-ordered validation set, not a random one; avoiding the leak.

Feature importance & SHAP

Gain/cover vs SHAP values for honest, per-prediction attribution — and spotting features that are leaks in disguise.

Predictions to positions

Turning predicted probabilities/returns into sized, leak-free signals for quantlab.backtest.
