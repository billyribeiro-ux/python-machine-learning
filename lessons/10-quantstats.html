Module 10 · Backtesting

Backtesting & QuantStats Tearsheets

Turn a returns series into a professional tearsheet, and learn the metrics that matter — plus walk-forward testing and the overfitting traps that invalidate most strategies.

🚧 Full lesson in progress

The course is being written module-by-module for maximum depth. This lesson's detailed outline is below so you can see exactly what it will cover — and the underlying quantlab package code it builds on already exists and is tested. Modules 0–4 are complete and runnable; work through those first.

What this lesson will cover

The full metric set

Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, max drawdown, exposure, tail ratio, VaR — building on quantlab.backtest.stats which already computes the core ones.

QuantStats tearsheets

qs.reports.html()/full() against a benchmark (SPY), and how to feed it our backtest output.

Walk-forward & out-of-sample

Train on a window, test on the next, roll forward — the only honest way to estimate live performance.

Overfitting & the multiple-testing problem

Why the best of 10,000 swept combos is probably luck; deflated Sharpe and the reality check.

Transaction costs & capacity

How fees, slippage, and market impact turn a paper edge into a real (or negative) one.
