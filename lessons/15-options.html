Module 15 · Putting It Together

Options: Chains, IV & Greeks

Bring options into QuantLab: pull chains through the provider, compute implied volatility and greeks, and scan for setups.

🚧 Full lesson in progress

The course is being written module-by-module for maximum depth. This lesson's detailed outline is below so you can see exactly what it will cover — and the underlying quantlab package code it builds on already exists and is tested. Modules 0–4 are complete and runnable; work through those first.

What this lesson will cover

Options data via the provider

get_options_chain (already implemented on YahooProvider) returns a tidy calls+puts frame tagged by type and expiry.

Black-Scholes & implied volatility

Pricing, solving for IV, and the greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega) from scratch — vectorized in NumPy.

The volatility surface

IV across strikes/expiries, skew, and term structure; building and visualizing the surface.

Simple options strategies

Covered calls, verticals, and their payoff/risk profiles, evaluated with our stats engine.

Options scanners

Screening for high-IV-rank premium selling or cheap convexity, ranked across underlyings.
