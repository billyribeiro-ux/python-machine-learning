Module 17 · Putting It Together

Testing & Quality at Scale

Make correctness a habit: known-value indicator tests, property-based testing, backtest invariants, and the full multi-indicator stats regression that guards your edge.

🚧 Full lesson in progress

The course is being written module-by-module for maximum depth. This lesson's detailed outline is below so you can see exactly what it will cover — and the underlying quantlab package code it builds on already exists and is tested. Modules 0–4 are complete and runnable; work through those first.

What this lesson will cover

Known-value & cross-implementation tests

What tests/test_indicators.py does: match our indicators to pandas and (optionally) TA-Lib, ignoring warmup NaNs.

Property-based testing with Hypothesis

Assert invariants over RANDOM inputs: RSI always in [0,100], ATR >= 0, SMA within [min,max] of its window, no look-ahead.

Backtest invariants

The leak guard (tests/test_strategy_stats.py): one-bar execution delay, costs only reduce returns, drawdown <= 0, equity reconciles with total return.

Regression-testing the stats

Pin the combo strategy's stats on fixed synthetic data so a refactor that changes results fails loudly.

CI & coverage

Running pytest on every change, skipping heavy/optional deps cleanly with importorskip, and keeping the suite fast and offline.
