Module 9 · Backtesting

vectorbt: Combine Indicators at Scale

vectorbt turns backtesting into array math: test thousands of indicator/parameter combinations at once and get a stats grid. This is the engine for 'combine multiple indicators with custom settings and come up with the stats' at industrial scale.

🚧 Full lesson in progress

The course is being written module-by-module for maximum depth. This lesson's detailed outline is below so you can see exactly what it will cover — and the underlying quantlab package code it builds on already exists and is tested. Modules 0–4 are complete and runnable; work through those first.

What this lesson will cover

From our engine to vectorbt

Map the leak-free model from quantlab.backtest onto Portfolio.from_signals — same rules (decide on t, act on t+1), vastly more throughput.

IndicatorFactory

Wrap any of our indicators so vectorbt can sweep their parameters across a grid (e.g. all SMA fast/slow pairs) in one vectorized run.

Parameter sweeps & combinations

Cartesian products of settings across multiple indicators, producing a multi-index stats table (Sharpe, return, drawdown per combo).

Reading the stats grid

Heatmaps of Sharpe over parameter space, and the danger of picking the single best cell (overfitting — Module 10/13).

Costs, slippage, and position sizing

Realistic fills, fees, and sizing so the swept stats mean something.
